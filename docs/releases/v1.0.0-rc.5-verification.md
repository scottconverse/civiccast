## Source

rc.5 is rc.4 plus three fixes, each merged by pull request with required CI passing:

- **#302:** live captions keep priority over programme preparation (FT-7).
- **#303:** media intake refuses XML documents (CVE-2026-120210); triage of the other November 6 FFmpeg advisories.
- **#305:** FFmpeg times are written as plain decimals, and a failed programme change is retried after the outgoing programme ends (FT-8).

On #305 the public-portal accessibility job stalled installing browsers and was cancelled at its time limit; one rerun passed. #304 set the version, and #306 filled in the signed-package details.

New tests for each fix failed on rc.4 source and pass on the fix. Local suites are listed in the release notes.

## 24-hour soak on the Beelink

**The 24-hour soak met the pass bar.**

The pass bar was: captions on time (4–6 s target, none over 10 s apart from recovered spikes), no hand-over failures, no worker restarts, no errors.

- **When:** the soak ran on the Beelink (the long-running test station, rc.5 installed by its published upgrade path) from about 15:54 UTC on November 7, 2026. It passed 24 hours at about 15:54 UTC on November 8.
- **Still running:** at the owner's request the soak continues with no end date. Results after 24 hours will be added later.
- **Service:** the station service ran continuously for the whole 24 hours, with no restart and the same process throughout.
- **Across the 24 hours:**
  - 0 playout-worker restarts;
  - 0 hand-over failures (F7);
  - 0 slate;
  - 0 control-plane errors;
  - no outage seen in any field check or in the on-station logger's record (the monitoring gap below had no minute-by-minute outage tracking).
- **Captions:** measured in 30-minute field checks, each with a 2-minute capture of all three channels compared with speech.
  - The average was about 3.3–3.8 s per check.
  - The slowest 5 % of words were within about 5.3 s.
  - Exactly one word was over 10 s in the whole period: 10.07 s on the government channel at 09:25 UTC on November 8, one of 135 words matched in that check. It was isolated and did not recur. That counts as a recovered spike under the pass bar.
  - The station's own caption self-check passed about 98 % (1,254 of 1,284 samples in the checks from the soak start to 14:37 UTC on November 8).
- **On-station logger:** a read-only logger sampling every 10 s was added at 05:58 UTC on November 8. From then on it recorded:
  - 100 % of minutes on air with a fresh picture on all three channels;
  - health and live captions healthy in every minute;
  - CPU averaging about 20 %;
  - free memory flat at about 33 GB of 48 GB;
  - brief frame-rate dips of about 5 seconds, mostly at programme changes (minute averages stayed at 30 fps).
- **Monitoring gap:** no field checks ran from 17:26 UTC on November 7 to 05:45 UTC on November 8, and the on-station logger did not yet exist. For that span:
  - the station's own logs show 0 worker restarts, 0 hand-over failures, 0 slate and 0 control-plane errors;
  - caption delay and self-check results for it were not captured and cannot be recovered.
- **Comparison:** r7 (rc.4, same checks) stayed on air with no restarts. It showed more caption-timing outliers, about 15–20 times more frame-rate-dip minutes, and far more refused caption publishes than the Beelink.
