# v1.0.0-rc.7 — 48-hour station soak results

Measured: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-21. Version reported by every logged row: `1.0.0-rc.7`.
Source data: `C:\CivicCastTester\soak-evidence-rc7-2026-11-21\<station>\soak-logger-data\minute-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`.

Four GPU stations each ran a three-channel station (public, education, government) for 48 hours.
Each station's `end-soak-status.txt` records reaching 48 hours and then `SERVICE=Stopped`, so the
service run time is 48 h for all four.

## Method

- Only rows whose `svc_pid` equals that station's soak service PID were used. These files also
  contain rows from earlier runs, which are excluded:

  | Station | Soak service PID |
  |---|---|
  | Beelink 64 GB | 7468 |
  | R9 | 18768 |
  | NVIDIA 5060 Ti | 15724 |
  | NVIDIA 5070 Ti | 25244 |

- **Health %** — share of logged minutes with `health = healthy`.
- **Captions %** — share of logged minutes with `live_captions = healthy`; other values are listed
  with their counts.
- **Channel ON_AIR %** — share of logged minutes with `<ch>_state = ON_AIR`, `<ch>_not_on_air = 0`
  and `<ch>_slate = 0`. A row that reports `ON_AIR` but still carries a non-zero `not_on_air` or
  `slate` in that minute is not counted.
- **Restarts** — `pub_restarts`, `edu_restarts` and `gov_restarts` are per-minute counts, not
  running totals: the column returns to 0 in the following logged minute, so the total is the sum
  of all rows in the window. See "Restarts" below.
- The final-24-hours window is the last 1440 logged minutes of each run. Every run logged
  consecutive minutes with no gaps, and the row count matches the span exactly.

The 5060 Ti's own clock runs 1 hour behind the host that wrote its status file: the station logged
19:53–19:49, while `end-soak-status.txt` reports the 48-hour mark at 20:50:58. This is a clock
offset in the station's logs, not a shorter run.

## Restarts

The restart columns are per-minute counts. Every non-zero value in all four runs occurred in the
opening hours of the run — mainly the startup minutes, when channels were still reaching
`ON_AIR`, plus a cluster of public-channel restarts during the 5060 Ti's and 5070 Ti's first two to
three hours. No channel on any station logged a restart after 2026-11-20 00:54, and none in the
final 24 hours.

## Limits

- These are four GPU stations (Beelink 64 GB, R9, NVIDIA 5060 Ti, NVIDIA 5070 Ti), each running
  three channels on rc.7. They are not the 16 GB no-GPU target configuration.
- The Dell and MSI stations are not part of this record.
- The figures describe what the minute logger recorded. They do not establish what a viewer saw,
  and they are not an installed-acceptance receipt.
- The per-minute logger samples once a minute; a fault shorter than one sampling interval can
  exist without appearing in these numbers.
