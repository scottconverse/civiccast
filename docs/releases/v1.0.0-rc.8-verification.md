## Station soak (more than 36 hours on four stations, not 48)

Figures were read from each station's own minute log and from its live channel status at **1:26 PM MST on November 23, 2026**. The stations were still running and still on air at that reading, so this is a reading, not an end time. All three channels played real video on every station.

"On air in one stretch" is the live `seconds_on_air` of each channel (it starts over after any dip). Worker restarts are the logger's "playout worker restarted" count per channel (public / education / government). Slate minutes are minutes in which a channel showed the slate. "Minutes logged" counts only minutes that ran this build, `1.0.0-rc.8`.

What the numbers do and do not say:

- **Four stations were on air for 36 hours or more in one stretch.** The Beelink was restarted by hand from its desktop at 3:43 PM on November 22 (not a fault of the build). Its 21.6 hours are the time since that restart. It had been up about 16 hours before it.
- **Worker restarts and slate minutes cluster at the start.** On the 5060 Ti, R9 and Beelink, the restarts and almost all slate minutes fall in the first hours after the build was installed (the evening of November 21 into the early morning of November 22), plus the Beelink's two restarts at its manual restart. After about 1 AM on November 22 (5060 Ti clock) the 5070 Ti, 5060 Ti and R9 logged no restart and no slate minute. The cause of the early restarts was not investigated for this record.
- **The 5060 Ti's clock is one hour behind the others.** Times in its log are one hour earlier than real time.
- **The MSI (16 GB) shows 1 to 2 slate minutes in most hours** through the whole run, plus a burst of restarts in the first hours of November 22, 498 minutes with no log row (the logger was not writing), and the service process id changed twice. Its lowest free memory was 1.5 GB and its CPU peaked at 96.7%. It stayed on air, but it is below the minimum and this is not proof of a supported machine.
- **The R9 reports 25.8 GB to Windows.** That is less than 32 GB. It probably has 32 GB installed with part reserved for its integrated graphics, but this was not checked. Do not count it as proof of the 32 GB minimum. The Beelink (47.8 GB, integrated AMD graphics, captions on) is the one integrated-graphics station with more than 32 GB reported.
- **"Not on air" counts in the raw logs are not outage minutes.** The logger counts every log line that reports a channel moving to a state other than ON_AIR (for example at a program change). These counts are large on some stations (hundreds) while the channels stayed on air in one stretch for 36 hours or more, so they are not used here.
- **Watchdog firings are not counted by the logger.** The logger counts worker restarts and slate minutes instead. A figure for watchdog firings is not given.
- **The 5070 Ti watcher marks its GPU "degraded" for every minute.** Each event says "GPU 0 throttle active: 0x400". Over the run the GPU peaked at 25% load, 47.4 °C and 40.3 W with 2.3 of 16.3 GB of video memory in use. What bit 0x400 means was not looked up. It did not coincide with any slate minute on that station.
- **Machine and CPU:** the 5070 Ti's CPU peaked at 86.3% and its lowest free memory was 10.5 GB. The 5060 Ti's CPU peaked at 89.6% (lowest free memory 15.1 GB), the R9's at 89.5% (15.7 GB) and the Beelink's at 76.4% (31.9 GB).

A sixth machine, a 16 GB laptop (Intel i7-9750H, GTX 1660 Ti 6 GB, captions off), was clean-installed with this build on November 23. Its three channels took 40 to 60 minutes after install to reach real video (first-program preparation was slow on its CPU). Then, from 12:03 to 12:33 PM MST, it showed 0 slate minutes on all three channels and was healthy in every minute. It is below the minimum, was watched for 30 minutes only, and is not proof of a supported machine.

Scope limits, stated plainly:

- This is a soak of **about 36 to 39 hours on four stations and about 22 hours on a fifth, not a 48-hour soak**.
- **No Intel integrated-graphics station was tested.**
- The MSI and the laptop are 16 GB machines, **below the new 32 GB minimum**. They are not proof of a supported machine.

## Minimum machine (owner decision)

The supported minimum is **32 GB of RAM**, with a processor whose integrated graphics are enough. No separate graphics card is required. This supersedes any earlier statement that no minimum hardware specification was published. Model-tier memory figures (for example the 16 GB that the 12B summary model needs) are model requirements and are unchanged.

## Open item: OpenSSL

- OpenSSL 3.5.9 has security advisories dated November 17, 2026.
- The fix version, 3.5.10, had no downloadable release when checked on November 22, 2026.
- Review result (2026-11-22, against the exact shipped files): **29 advisory findings**, **28 not reachable** from the shipped programs, plugins and code, **1 not proven**: `gstreamer-openssl 3.5.9` + `CVE-2026-95690`. `gioopenssl.dll` contains the DTLS entry points; no shipped program, plugin, or CivicCast code was found that calls them; shipped PyGObject could construct a DTLS connection; this was NOT proven at run time; the owner accepted this for rc.8 on 2026-11-22; replace with OpenSSL 3.5.10 when it is released.
- The evidence is **static file evidence only** — import tables, string tables and source read from the shipped files. Nothing was run. The fix ships in a later build when OpenSSL 3.5.10 exists.
- The 29 findings are triaged individually in `security/native-audit-triage.json` (reviewed 2026-11-22, review by 2026-12-22).
