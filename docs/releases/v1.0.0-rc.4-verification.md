# RC4 preparation and verification limits

**v1.0.0-rc.4 is an unpublished candidate.** This record describes source
preparation as of November 2, 2026. It does not identify a signed RC4 package or
establish that the candidate is ready for distribution. The current published
download remains [v1.0.0-rc.3](https://github.com/scottconverse/civiccast-native/releases/tag/v1.0.0-rc.3);
its [package record](v1.0.0-rc.3-verification.md) retains its own evidence and limits.

The candidate source contains the following focused corrections:

- Preserve fractional repeat durations when deriving scheduled source boundaries.
- Prefer the selected frame's media-wall timestamp for source proofs, as-run
  starts and program-label transitions, with a fallback when that timestamp is
  unavailable or malformed.
- Bound report airtime by the next observed source on the same channel, retaining
  historical ledger rows and the distinction between completed and provisional
  open records. Different channels remain additive.
- Capture caption proof from the advertised local HLS output and distinguish an
  unavailable capture from a decoder failure or a stale sample.
- Correct the operator's batch caption request body. The installed RC3
  “Caption selected” action returned HTTP 422 because the client encoded its
  JSON body twice. The corrected RC4 UI and caption-retention acceptance are
  pending; no API workaround is counted as installed acceptance.
- Keep Schedule approval and conflict times in the page's configured station
  time zone. The reused RC3 package displayed the exact 38-second interval but
  used the browser clock in its approval panel while its list used station
  time. Final RC4 installed verification remains pending.

These are source changes. Focused checks establish only the paths they exercise;
they do not establish installed behavior, speech-caption synchronization, caption
coverage, ASR accuracy or a continuous three-channel soak. Historical R7/R9
observations remain historical and are not retrospectively marked passed.

The source also corrects the caption receipt's treatment of encoder lookahead
at a real program reset. An outgoing EOC at or after the incoming cutoff is
retired rather than claimed displayed. Genuine backwards clocks remain errors.
Eighteen focused clock and engine-wiring checks passed; final installed evidence
is pending. This receipt correction does not reduce recognition latency or
establish accurate transcription.

The reused installed PR291 sample showed matching Public A/B picture and labels
with first-picture delays of 8.468 and 8.659 seconds from selected output. All
three advertised HLS clocks mapped through the transition with maximum sampled
error below 0.001 seconds. Independent machine word references for short saved
speech windows estimated sampled page delays of approximately 7–15 seconds;
transcription substitutions and outgoing captions retired at the program boundary
remain explicit limitations. These word timestamps are not calibrated human
ground truth. The historical R9 black-picture condition remains unclassified;
the new Public sample's absence of black frames does not resolve its cause.
